Conclusion

In seven months I've moved from learning the Zoho and M-Files platforms to delivering applications ABITS uses every day: Zoho Expense, SWOC 2.0 and 3.0 in Zoho CRM, and the Equipment Maintenance app. Along the way I earned the M-Files Solution Engineer certification with a score of 90/100.

Alongside development, I've taken on functional work: client demos, MOMs, proposals and SRS documents, approvals and follow-ups. I also gave Zoho One KT to new joiners, and every session was rated 10/10.

My commitment to ABITS is to take full ownership of implementations, deliver them on time, and keep bringing practical automation to our clients and teams.

Lessons learned

  • A clear requirement up front saves rework later
  • AI output needs a review step before it reaches business data
  • Testing scheduled and email workflows takes patience and method
  • Good documentation (MOMs, SRS) keeps clients and the team aligned
  • Teaching a tool is the best way to master it
